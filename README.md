## ⚙️ 2. Core Mechanics, Dynamics, Aesthetics & Bartle Types

| # | ⚙️ Mechanic | 🔄 Dynamic | 🎨 Aesthetic | 👤 Bartle Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun Currency Collection | Players collect Sun and decide when to save or spend it on plants. | Challenge | Achiever |
| 2 | Grid-Based Placement | Players choose suitable tiles and arrange plants across the lawn. | Expression | Achiever |
| 3 | Cooldown Timers | Players wait for plants to recharge and plan their next placement. | Challenge | Achiever |
| 4 | Lane Defense System | Players build defenses in different lanes according to incoming zombies. | Challenge | Achiever |
| 5 | Lawn Mower Backup | Players use the mower as an emergency backup when zombies reach the end of a lane. | Challenge | Achiever |
| 6 | Plant & Zombie Combat | Players combine attacking and defensive plants to stop approaching zombies. | Fantasy | Achiever |
| 7 | Defensive Stalling | Players place Wall-nuts to slow zombies while attacking plants deal damage. | Challenge | Explorer |
